**Product:** White-label AI SaaS platform for agencies

## 📈 Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Launch (Months 1-3)

**Goal:** 10 paying customers

**Tactics:**
- Build landing page with demo
- Content marketing (blog, SEO)
- LinkedIn outreach to agencies
- Product Hunt launch
- Beta program (50% off for first 10 customers)

**Budget:** $5K (mostly ads + content)

### Phase 2: Traction (Months 4-12)

**Goal:** 50 customers ($15K MRR)

**Tactics:**
- Paid ads (Google, LinkedIn)
- Agency partnership program
- Webinar series
- Case studies from beta customers
- Referral program (1 month free per referral)

**Budget:** $20K/month (ads + content + events)

### Phase 3: Scale (Year 2)

**Goal:** 200 customers ($80K MRR)

**Tactics:**
- Hire sales team (2 AEs)
- Customer success manager
- Agency accelerator program
- Conference sponsorships
- Integration partnerships (HubSpot, Shopify)

**Budget:** $100K/month (team + ads + events)

### Phase 4: Growth (Year 3)

**Goal:** 500 customers ($250K MRR)

**Tactics:**
- Expand sales team (5 AEs)
- International expansion (EU, Asia)
- Enterprise sales team
- Acquisition of smaller competitors
- Prepare for exit

**Budget:** $300K/month (team + expansion)

## ⚠️ Risk Mitigation

### Technical Risks

**Risk:** Platform downtime  
**Mitigation:** 99.9% SLA, multi-region deployment, monitoring

**Risk:** Data breach  
**Mitigation:** Encryption, regular audits, compliance (GDPR, SOC2)

**Risk:** AI provider dependency  
**Mitigation:** Multi-provider support (OpenClaw, OpenAI, Anthropic)

### Market Risks

**Risk:** Competition  
**Mitigation:** Focus on agency niche, superior white-label features

**Risk:** Market saturation  
**Mitigation:** International expansion, vertical-specific features

**Risk:** Economic downturn  
**Mitigation:** Essential service positioning, flexible pricing

### Financial Risks

**Risk:** High churn  
**Mitigation:** Customer success team, product-led growth

**Risk:** Slow growth  
**Mitigation:** Pivot pricing, double down on winning channels

**Risk:** Cash flow  
**Mitigation:** Annual prepay discounts, 6-month runway minimum

## 📊 Key Metrics (KPIs)

- **Server Uptime** (target: 99.9%)
- **API Response Time** (target: <200ms)
- **Error Rate** (target: <0.1%)
